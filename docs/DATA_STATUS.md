**GLKN: preparation + QA complete, final analysis boundary PENDING.** Geometry repair + standardization + HUC geography QA all done (`docs/data_inventory.md` §14, `outputs/qa/glkn_processing_report.md`). Geometry repair is clean (0.0% area change across all 7 parks). The Isle Royale/Voyageurs non-standard HUC code investigation (§14.7) established that those codes correspond 100% to the Canadian portion of the monitoring landscape (`loc_02=='canada'`, zero exceptions) — explains *why* no standard HUC code exists (USGS WBD doesn't cover Canada) but not what the codes themselves mean; paused per instruction rather than pursued further. **Standard US HUC boundaries alone are insufficient for ISRO/VOYA analysis regions** since their attributed data extend into Canada, where no boundary geometry currently exists in this project. Per-park dissolved analysis-boundary work (HUC10-based landscape containers) is deferred — not blocking the rest of the reference-data assessment.

**ADS R6: simplified preparation + QA complete.** Uses `BugNet_R6` EPA ecoregion boundaries directly as analysis regions (dissolved 19 state-fragments → 7 clean ecoregions) rather than reconstructing ADS's historical study design (`docs/data_inventory.md` §15, `outputs/qa/ads_r6_processing_report.md`). Headline: 97.0% of ADS R6's attributed area falls inside the 7-ecoregion union; damage concentration and causal-agent mix vary meaningfully by ecoregion. Two data-quality items caught and fixed along the way: a >12-minute-and-killed naive overlay (fixed with a prepared-geometry short-circuit, now ~6 min) and a per-ecoregion area bug producing impossible >100% fill fractions (fixed; two ecoregions still legitimately exceed 100% due to the 29-year repeated-survey design, verified not a bug).

**ADS R10: simplified preparation + QA complete.** Uses `BugNet_R10` HUC6 watershed boundaries directly as analysis regions (already one row per HUC6, no dissolve needed) rather than reconstructing ADS's historical study design (`docs/data_inventory.md` §16, `outputs/qa/ads_r10_processing_report.md`). Headline: 90.5% of ADS R10's attributed area falls inside the 20-HUC6 union (lower than R6's 97.0%); damage concentration and causal-agent mix vary strongly by HUC6, with a clear bark-beetle-dominated south/coastal vs. defoliator/leafminer-dominated north/interior split. Raw zipped GDB extracted to a persistent working copy at `data/processed/ads_r10/AK_Region10_AllYears.gdb` (mirroring the GLKN precedent); extraction also surfaced official USDA schema documentation, relocated to `docs/source_docs/ads/IDS_FlatFiles_Readme.pdf`. One bug caught and fixed: this GDB export has no `OBJECTID` column (unlike the readme's generic description implied); `DAMAGE_AREA_ID` used as row identifier instead.

## Answers to the standing questions

- **What NCCN data do we actually have?** All three current-schema parks (MORA, NOCA, OLYM) plus LEWI, plus two superseded legacy files (NOCA-V2B, OLYM-V2B). All inspected, all now under `data/raw/nccn/`.
- **What GLKN/Glicken data do we actually have?** A real, large, already-certified dataset (177,153 features, 7 parks) at `data/raw/glkn/LandTrendr` — it was sitting in a misnamed (extension-less) File Geodatabase, not empty as first thought.
- **Do we have ADS R6?** Yes, one polygon-only shapefile export (913,165 features) at `data/raw/ads/r6/`. Likely missing the points/surveyed-extent layers that R10 has.
- **Do we have ADS R10?** Yes — at `data/raw/ads/r10/AK_Region10_AllYears.gdb.zip`, still zipped. Full GDB with 3 layers (damage areas, damage points, surveyed extent).
- **Which things are still zipped?** `data/raw/ads/r10/AK_Region10_AllYears.gdb.zip` (ADS R10), `data/raw/boundaries/nps/National_Parks.zip` (NPS boundaries), and `data/raw/boundaries/other/drive-download-...zip` (kept intact as the canonical source package for the BugNet/HUC6 boundaries, whose contents were separately extracted — see below).
- **Which ZIPs have already been extracted?** Only the BugNet/HUC6 boundary ZIP, and only once — its contents now live split across `data/raw/boundaries/ads_r6/` and `data/raw/boundaries/ads_r10/`, with the original ZIP still preserved intact in `data/raw/boundaries/other/`. `AK_Region10_AllYears.gdb.zip` and `National_Parks.zip` have not been extracted — both were inspected directly via GDAL's `/vsizip/`.
- **What geodatabases exist?** `data/raw/glkn/LandTrendr` (misnamed, no `.gdb` extension) and `data/raw/ads/r10/AK_Region10_AllYears.gdb.zip` (properly named, zipped).
- **What layers are inside each geodatabase?** GLKN: 1 spatial + 3 non-spatial admin tables. ADS R10: 3 spatial layers (areas, points, surveyed extent).
- **Which shapefiles are exports/copies of GDB layers?** `ADS_R6_Damage_allyears.shp` is almost certainly a shapefile export of a GDB layer equivalent to R10's `DAMAGE_AREAS_FLAT` (same field meanings, but truncated 10-character field names — a shapefile fingerprint). The original R6 GDB (if one exists) has not been provided.
- **What boundary datasets do we have?** National NPS unit boundaries (all 11 NCCN+GLKN target parks matched 1:1), EPA-ecoregion-based R6 candidate boundaries, HUC6-based R10 candidate boundaries (both under the generic name "BugNet"), and the full 38-basin Alaska HUC6 catalog — all under `data/raw/boundaries/`.
- **What boundary datasets are still missing?** GLKN HUC12 boundary polygons (only the attribute exists — but 230 of 247 codes are standard USGS WBD format and pullable now, see `docs/glkn_huc12_codes.txt`; 17 Isle-Royale-area codes are non-standard and need clarification from Al).
- **What has actually been inspected?** Everything listed with ✅ above — every field list, CRS, and feature count in this table came from actually opening the data, not from a filename. Every dataset was also re-verified readable after the 2026-09-22 reorganization move.
- **What have we received but not inspected?** Nothing outstanding as of this update — the only gaps left are things not yet received at all (❌ rows).
